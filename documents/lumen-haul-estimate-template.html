Terms

Validity

Holds for 30 days from the date on page one. An estimate, not a fixed price: the invoice follows the work done. Crew and equipment are booked only once it is approved.

Payment

On booking, 50% holds the date and books crew; the balance is due net 15 from delivery. Cheque or bank transfer.

Production day

Ten hours from crew call, meal break included. Overtime at 1.5× the hourly equivalent, agreed on the day before it is run.

Travel and expenses

Mileage beyond 50 miles of Redwood City, parking, permits, lodging and per diem are billed at cost with receipts.

Cancellation and reschedule

Cancelling more than 7 days out is free of charge; inside 7 days, 50% of the production subtotal; inside 48 hours, 100%, since crew and rentals are committed. One weather reschedule is free where the shoot depends on conditions.

Delivery and usage

Delivered files are licensed to the client for the agreed use. Raw footage stays with Lumen Haul unless a transfer is estimated.

Equipment and insurance

Owned packages are carried on Lumen Haul’s policy; sub‑rented gear is insured for the booking. Loss or damage caused by the client or the client’s location is billed at replacement cost.

Portfolio

Lumen Haul may show the finished work in its portfolio and on its channels. Tell us before booking if the project is under embargo.

Approval

Approve to proceed — nothing is held until then
