Terms

Validity and payment

Holds until 2 October 2026. 50% on acceptance to hold the date and book the stage, balance net 15 from final delivery.

Hours and overtime

Ten hours from call, meal break included. The day is built around eight subjects at four outfits each; more of either needs a second day rather than overtime. Overtime at 1.5× the hourly equivalent, agreed on the day, and stage overtime at the studio’s own rate.

Location and travel

San Francisco, Los Angeles or Chicago. San Francisco carries no travel charge; the other two are billed at cost with receipts, agreed in writing first.

Studio booking

The stage is held once this quote is accepted, subject to availability. Rates differ by city, so the line on page 1 is confirmed at booking.

Delivery and turnaround

A gallery for selection within three working days. Edited finals within ten working days of your selection. The commercial follows within three weeks.

Cancellation and reschedule

Free more than 7 days out; inside 7 days, 50% of the production subtotal; inside 48 hours, 100%. Any stage deposit follows the studio’s own terms.

Revisions

Two rounds on the stills and two on the commercial are included. Further rounds are billed at $700 a day, in half day increments, agreed before they start.

Stills usage

Unlimited use of the delivered stills for marketing, press and social, worldwide and in perpetuity. Raw files stay with Lumen Haul.

Commercial usage

The finished commercial is cleared for brand channels and paid social, worldwide, for twelve months from delivery. Broadcast, cinema or out‑of‑home placement is a separate license, as is renewal past the term.

Music

Delivered with a licensed track covering the usage above. Swapping the track, or extending the license beyond it, is quoted separately.

Portfolio

Lumen Haul may show selected stills and the commercial in its portfolio once the work is public.

Equipment and insurance

Lighting and camera packages are carried on Lumen Haul’s policy. A certificate of insurance is issued to the stage on request.
